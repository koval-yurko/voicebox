Voicebox: project map

A local-first voice studio. It makes speech (TTS), writes down speech (STT), and gives voice to agents. This custom build (0.6.0-custom.1) adds real-time streaming TTS, streaming STT, and LiveKit plugins. Click the items on each tab.

Streaming STT: how the 3 adapters differ

Audio frames arrive over WS /stt/ws. A Silero VAD cuts the audio into segments. The adapter for the chosen model makes the text. Pick an adapter.

Events sent to the client

readyspeech_startinterimfinalspeech_enderror

The text field is always the full segment text. The client replaces; it never appends. stable_text is the part that will not change.
